What Aladdin is, who it's for, and what happens the moment you use it

What Is Aladdin?

Think of a personal talent scout — you describe exactly what you want, and they spend all day scouring the entire job market, bringing back only the listings that actually fit. Aladdin is that scout, built for entry-level software engineers who are tired of wading through "Senior" roles that clog every job board.

It's a full-stack AI job search engine that combines job aggregation, resume analysis, AI writing tools, and application tracking into one opinionated system. No accounts scattered across five sites. One place.

🎯
Job Discovery
Automatically discovers jobs from 6 sources every few minutes — no manual searching required.
⭐
Smart Scoring
Rates each job 0–100 based on your actual resume, so the best matches float to the top.
✉️
Cover Letters
Generates tailored cover letters in seconds — word by word, streamed live as you watch.
📄
Resume Tailoring
Rewrites your resume to highlight the skills that match each specific job description.
📋
Application Tracking
A drag-and-drop Kanban board tracks every application's status at a glance.

The Problem It Solves

The traditional job search is designed for people who already have experience — not for new grads. Aladdin flips that by automating every tedious part and ruthlessly filtering out the noise.

😩 The Old Way
⏳ Scroll job boards for hours — most listings are "Senior" roles you can't apply to
✍️ Write every cover letter from scratch — 30 minutes each, multiplied by every application
📊 Track applications in a chaotic spreadsheet that quickly becomes a mess
✨ Aladdin's Way
🤖 Auto-discovers entry-level jobs 24/7 — Senior roles are automatically deleted before you ever see them
⚡ AI generates a tailored cover letter in seconds — customized to each job, not a generic template
📋 Kanban board tracks every application's stage — Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected — all in one place
🔪
The Ruthless Cleanup Filter

Auto-deletes any job with "Senior", "Lead", "Manager", or "Director" in the title. False negatives (missing a good job) are acceptable. False positives (showing you a bad job) are not.

The Design Insight

The whole flow is built around one idea: AI is most useful when it has rich context. Every step — preferences, resume, job description — feeds more context into the AI, making the next output sharper.

The Tech Stack at a Glance

Aladdin is built from 5 key technologies. You don't need to understand each one deeply — just know what role it plays. A tech stack is just the list of tools an app uses.

⚡
Next.js

The framework that runs both the website AND the server — two-for-one, one codebase.

🗄️
PostgreSQL

The database that stores all jobs, resumes, and applications — the app's permanent memory.

🤖
OpenRouter / Gemini

The AI provider that writes cover letters and tailors resumes — the intelligence behind the writing tools.

🔐
Clerk

Handles login, passwords, and sessions — so the app doesn't have to build that security from scratch.

☁️
AWS S3

Stores uploaded PDF files — resumes and cover letters — safely in the cloud.

🧭
Why This Matters for Steering AI

Knowing the stack transforms your instructions. Instead of "add a login page," you can say "add a Clerk-authenticated route." Precise vocabulary = better AI output, every time.
